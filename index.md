## About Me
I am a Research Intern at [Tencent Hunyuan](https://hunyuan.tencent.com/) (青云人才计划 / Qingyun Top Talent Program), where I build models for **omni-video understanding** — making sense of a video together with the audio and language that come with it (e.g., <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.11244">Script-a-Video</a>, deep structured audio-visual captioning).
Before that, I was a remote research intern at [Vision-CAIR, KAUST](https://vision-cair.kaust.edu.sa/), advised by [Mohamed Elhoseiny](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/people/person/mohamed-elhoseiny), and a research intern at [SenseTime](https://www.sensetime.com/en). I hold a B.S. in Mathematics (minor in Management) from Lanzhou University.

What I care about is moving models past *recognition* and into *understanding* — not just naming what appears in a scene, but reasoning about why it happens and what comes next, with the reasoning grounded in evidence from every modality and across time. The threads below are different angles on that same goal.

## Research Interests

- **Video, end to end:** long-form and audio-visual comprehension that fuses vision, audio, and language over time (<a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/svagent.github.io/">SVAgent<sub>CVPR</sub></a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.11244">Script&#8209;a&#8209;Video<sub>Tech&nbsp;Report</sub></a>)
- **Reasoning &amp; agents:** how models use — and sometimes fake — cross-modal evidence when reasoning about changing world states (<a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/UFO/">UFO<sub>ICML</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/inex.github.io/">InEx<sub>AAAI</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/mermaid.github.io/">MERMAID<sub>EMNLP</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/xr.github.io/">XR<sub>WWW</sub></a>)
- **Generation as evidence:** treating generated images, sequences, and future states as cues for understanding and prediction (<a href="https://wikiautogen.github.io/">WikiAutoGen<sub>ICCV</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/rechar.github.io/">ReChar<sub>SIGGRAPH&nbsp;Asia</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/metronet.github.io/">MeteoNet<sub>CVPR</sub></a>)
- **Efficiency:** compressing multimodal tokens so long, dense video stays tractable (<a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/script.github.io/">Script<sub>TMLR</sub></a>)

In the long run, I want to build general-purpose systems that perceive, reason, and communicate across vision, audio, language, and action in dynamic, real-world environments.
