## About Me
I am a Research Intern at [Tencent Hunyuan](https://hunyuan.tencent.com/) (青云人才计划 / Qingyun Top Talent Program), where I work on **omni-video understanding**.
Previously, I was a Remote Research Intern at [Vision-CAIR, KAUST](https://vision-cair.kaust.edu.sa/), advised by [Mohamed Elhoseiny](https://cemse.kaust.edu.sa/people/person/mohamed-elhoseiny), and a Research Intern at [SenseTime](https://www.sensetime.com/en). I received my B.S. with a major in Mathematics and a minor in Management from Lanzhou University, China.

My research centers on **omni-video understanding** — building unified models and agents that jointly **perceive, reason over, and generate from** video together with its audio, language, and visual context. I am driven by a single question: how can a model move beyond passive recognition to genuinely understand *what* is happening in a dynamic scene, *why* it happens, and *what comes next* — grounding its reasoning in evidence across every modality and across time.

## Research Interests

I pursue omni-video understanding along four connected threads:

- **Omni-modal Video Understanding:** long-form and audio-visual comprehension that fuses vision, audio, and language over time (<a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/svagent.github.io/">SVAgent<sub>CVPR</sub></a>, <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.11244">Script&#8209;a&#8209;Video<sub>Tech&nbsp;Report</sub></a>)
- **Compositional Multimodal Reasoning &amp; Agents:** testing and strengthening how models use cross-modal *evidence* to reason about evolving world states (<a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/UFO/">UFO<sub>ICML</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/inex.github.io/">InEx<sub>AAAI</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/mermaid.github.io/">MERMAID<sub>EMNLP</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/xr.github.io/">XR<sub>WWW</sub></a>)
- **Generative Cues &amp; World Models:** using generation — images, sequences, future states — as evidence for understanding and prediction (<a href="https://wikiautogen.github.io/">WikiAutoGen<sub>ICCV</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/rechar.github.io/">ReChar<sub>SIGGRAPH&nbsp;Asia</sub></a>, <a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/metronet.github.io/">MeteoNet<sub>CVPR</sub></a>)
- **Efficient Multimodal Modeling:** token compression that makes long, dense video tractable (<a href="https://01yzzyu.github.io/script.github.io/">Script<sub>TMLR</sub></a>)

My long-term goal is to build **general-purpose omni-video systems** that perceive, reason, and communicate across visual, audio, textual, and behavioral modalities in dynamic, real-world environments.
